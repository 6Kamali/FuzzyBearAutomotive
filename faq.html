Frequently Asked Questions

How far do you travel?

We serve all of Manitoba. While based in Alexander, we regularly travel to Winnipeg, Brandon, and Northern communities for specialized services.

Can you help if I lost all my keys?

Yes. We are experts in "All Keys Lost" situations. We cut and program new keys directly at your vehicle's location.

Do you work on push-to-start "Smart Keys"?

Absolutely. We carry a wide inventory of proximity fobs and smart keys for modern vehicles. We can program the remote entry, the push-start ignition, and even cut the emergency "hidden" blade inside the fob right in our mobile unit.

What is a "Red Seal" technician, and why does it matter?

Red Seal is the highest Canadian standard for skilled trades. It means your vehicle is being handled by a certified professional with decades of proven expertise, not an amateur with a YouTube degree. When it comes to your car's complex computer system, you want a certified expert.

Are you a Canadian company?

Yes, 100%. Fuzzy Bear Automotive is a proudly Canadian-owned and operated small business. We were founded in 2021 and are based right here in Alexander, MB. When you hire us, your money stays in the Manitoba economy.

Can you program keys for older or "Classic" cars?

We do! Beyond modern fobs, we specialize in custom wiring and ignition restoration for vintage vehicles. If your classic needs a specialized electrical solution or a hard-to-find key originated, we have the tools and the 30+ years of experience to handle it.

How do I pay for mobile service?

For your convenience, we accept major credit cards, debit, and e-transfers on-site. You'll receive a professional digital receipt for your records or insurance purposes immediately after the job is completed.
