Frequently Asked Questions

How far do you travel?

We serve all of Manitoba. While based in Alexander, we regularly travel to Winnipeg, Brandon, and Northern communities for specialized mobile dispatches.

Can you help if I lost all my keys?

Yes. We specialize in "All Keys Lost" situations. We use high-end decoding tools to cut and program new keys directly at your vehicle's location—no towing required.

Do you work on push-to-start "Smart Keys"?

Absolutely. We carry a wide inventory of proximity fobs and smart keys for modern vehicles. We can program the remote entry and the push-start ignition right in our mobile unit.

What is a "Red Seal" technician?

Red Seal is the highest Canadian standard for skilled trades. It ensures your vehicle is being handled by a certified professional with decades of proven expertise, not an amateur.

Are you a Canadian company?

Yes, 100%. Fuzzy Bear Automotive is a proudly Canadian-owned and operated small business, founded in 2021 and based right here in Alexander, MB.

Can you program keys for older or "Classic" cars?

We do! Beyond modern fobs, we specialize in custom wiring and ignition restoration for vintage vehicles. With 25+ years of experience, we can handle what dealers won't.

How do I pay for mobile service?

We accept major credit cards, debit, and e-transfers on-site. You'll receive a professional digital receipt for insurance purposes immediately after the job is completed.
