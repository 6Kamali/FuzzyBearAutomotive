Our Story

Founded in 2021, Fuzzy Bear Automotive was built to bridge the gap between overpriced dealerships and under-equipped local shops. We are a **proudly Canadian-owned and operated company**, rooted right here in Alexander, Manitoba.

We know that when your vehicle's security system fails or you lose your only set of keys, you aren't just looking for a technician—you're looking for a solution that doesn't involve a week-long wait and a massive towing bill. That’s why we built our business to be 100% Mobile. While our heart is in Alexander, our office is wherever your car is parked.
